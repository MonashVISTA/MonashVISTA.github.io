02.SMART HEALTH CARE

Vision-based Posture Correction Wearable System

Prolonged awkward back posture is a major cause of workplace injury, yet most posture wearables rely on body-worn sensors. This project implements an alternative approach. Two cameras track the wearer’s spine without contact through SpinePose-based spinal landmark estimation and a lightweight harness with eight vibration motors cues them when their posture drifts from their individually calibrated neutral reference. An AI model recognises whether the wearer is standing, sitting or lifting, so the feedback adapts to what they’re doing. The working prototype has shown the concept is technically feasible, with accuracy, response time and battery life to be refined in the future. This system is the Monash VISTA executive team’s final year project. Future team members will take on further development and refinement of the project.

03.ASSISTIVE AI

Interactive Capybara

The Interactive AI Capybara project focuses on developing an AI-powered virtual assistant that makes human-AI interaction more engaging and accessible. Featuring Stella the Capybara, the system combines large language models (LLMs) with holographic display technology to create an interactive character that users can communicate with through natural language. The assistant is designed to answer questions, provide directions and introduce visitors to projects at the Monash Smart Infrastructures Lab. The long-term goal is to explore how personality-driven AI and holographic interfaces can support more natural and engaging interactions in universities, exhibitions and other public spaces.

04.COMPUTER VISION

Edgesight AI inferencing (EAII)

EAII seeks to develop and streamline computer vision focused IoTs operating on the edge. The current project is focused on development of cameras equipped with multimodal AI systems such as object detection models and SLMs to generate semantic data. The long term goal is to engineer customisable, user-friendly devices that are safe, efficient and deployable.
