Why partner with us?

Access emerging talent

Through partnering with Monash VISTA your organisation will have direct access to emerging talent in engineering, computer science, AI, design and business.

Our members have hands-on R&D experience in computer vision, AI, IoT, and smart infrastructure as well as soft skills highly valued in the professional world.

By supporting Monash VISTA you will be investing in both potential future interns and employees as well as a pipeline of exceptional STEM talent.
